I've taught ocean physics, chemistry and marine biogeochemistry at undergraduate and postgraduate levels, supervised Masters students, and founded and contributed to programs that help students from underrepresented backgrounds into graduate research.

## Teaching

**Co-lecturer and unit co-coordinator, QMS512 Marine Biogeochemistry**<br>
*University of Tasmania, postgraduate · 2025, 2026*<br>
Co-designed the unit with Dr Tyler Rohr, and developed and presented around half of the lecture and tutorial material. In 2025, 11 of 13 responding students rated the unit "Excellent" or "Very Good"; in 2026, responding students gave 100% agreement on all teaching-quality criteria.

**Teaching Assistant, SIO 119 Physics and Chemistry of the Ocean**<br>
*UC San Diego, undergraduate · 2022, 2023*<br>
Ran weekly discussion sections and presented lecture material for third- and fourth-year students (enrolments of 83 and 110). Across both years, 97% of student evaluations rated my teaching "Excellent" or "Above Average" (79% "Excellent"), and 97% would recommend me to other students.

**Introduction to College Teaching**<br>
*UC San Diego · 2020*<br>
A 10-week course in student-centred learning and equitable teaching practices.

## Supervision

**Master of Advanced Studies capstone projects**<br>
*UC San Diego · 2021, 2022*<br>
Supervised three students to graduation, providing project guidance, feedback, and coding and data-analysis support. Projects spanned humpback whale migration pathways, the international law of the sea, and communicating science through immersive performance.

## Mentoring and inclusion

**Founder, Scripps Applicant Support and Knowledgebase**<br>
*Scripps Institution of Oceanography · 2021–2024*<br>
Founded and directed a program pairing PhD applicants from underrepresented backgrounds with graduate student mentors through the application process. I personally mentored five applicants.

**Mentor, Científico Latino Graduate Student Mentorship Initiative**<br>
*2020*<br>
Mentored three students through graduate school and fellowship applications, two of them successfully.

**Member, Inclusion, Diversity, Equity and Accessibility Committee**<br>
*Institute for Marine and Antarctic Studies · 2024–present*<br>
Organise events promoting mental health awareness and prepare the committee's monthly digest.

**Secretary, Scripps Graduate Student Council**<br>
*Scripps Institution of Oceanography · 2020–2021*<br>
Represented the graduate student body to Scripps leadership.
